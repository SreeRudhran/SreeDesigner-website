"Kundan is the jewel of Indian royal courts—faceted, luminous, and timeless. When paired with the antique gold borders of a Kanchipuram wedding saree, handcrafted kundan embroidery catches the flickering light of sacred homam flames like glowing gems."

1. The Royal Heritage of Kundan & Jadau Embroidery in South Indian Bridal Blouses

Historically reserved for imperial royal vestments in Rajasthan and Mysore palaces, kundan embroidery has become an indispensable hallmark of modern South Indian brides in 2026. Unlike flat machine rhinestones that look cheap under photography, authentic cut-glass kundan stones have beveled facets and metallic foil backing that reflect warm, multidirectional brilliance.

2. Key Kundan Stone Shapes & Their Placement on Bridal Silk Blouses

1) Teardrop (Thilakam) Kundan: Framed in French coil wire around deep back necklines; 2) Navratna Multi-Stone Clusters: Traditional 9-gem sacred alignments placed at shoulder caps; 3) Uncut Jadau Floral Rosettes: Clustered along elbow sleeve borders; 4) Emerald & Ruby Baguette Kundan: Geometric necklines framing antique temple necklaces.

4. Preventing Stone Detachment: Sree Designers 4-Point Prongs & Double Threading

Cheap stones are hot-glued and fall off midway through wedding rituals. At Sree Designers, every single kundan stone is hand-anchored through dual-prong metallic settings and secured underneath with double-twisted nylon-waxed thread.

Frequently Asked Questions by Brides

Will kundan stones on the sleeves scratch my skin or catch on the saree pallu? +

Never. We hand-buff every metal bezel and encase the inner sleeve seams in velvet piping so neither your arms nor delicate silk zari catches.

Do kundan stones lose their shine or turn cloudy after storage? +

We exclusively use authentic foil-backed glass kundan with anti-oxidation coating that remains luminous for decades.

Can you match the kundan stones to my specific bridal necklace set? +

Yes! Bring a photograph or swatch of your bridal jewelry to your fitting consultation, and we will color-match ruby, emerald, and polki stones.

Can a heavy kundan blouse be altered if my weight changes after the wedding? +

Yes! We build generous 2.5-inch hidden side seam margins into every blouse for effortless future adjustments.
