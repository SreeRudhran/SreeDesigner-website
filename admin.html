Google Merchant Center

Free Shopping Tab & Image Search Listings

Submit this XML URL in Google Merchant Center under Products > Feeds > Add Primary Feed. Google will fetch your new designs every day.

https://sreedesigners.in/google-merchant-feed.xml
Step-by-step setup:
1. Sign in to Google Merchant Center.
2. Go to Products → Feeds → Primary Feeds (+).
3. Select country India, language English.
4. Choose Scheduled fetch and paste the copied URL.

Pinterest Product Catalogs

Turn Blouses into Shoppable Product Pins

Pinterest supports Google Merchant Center XML feeds natively. Add this Data Source to automatically turn your blouses into Shoppable Pins.

https://sreedesigners.in/google-merchant-feed.xml
Step-by-step setup:
1. Sign in to Pinterest Business Hub.
2. Go to Ads / Business → Catalogs → Add data source.
3. Paste the feed URL, select INR currency, and choose daily sync.
4. Pinterest will generate Shoppable Product Pins with live prices automatically!
