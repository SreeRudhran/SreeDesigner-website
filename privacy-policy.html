Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, India)

1. Commitment to Your Privacy

At Sree Designers & Boutique ("we," "our," or "us"), we deeply value the trust you place in us when sharing your bespoke bridal requirements, measurements, and personal contact details. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, protect, and process your information across our website (sreedesigners.in) and our studio communication channels, in strict compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to fulfill our custom tailoring, embroidery, and boutique services:

  • Personal Contact Information: Name, phone / WhatsApp number, and delivery address provided when booking a trial fitting, requesting an estimate, or ordering a bridal blouse.
  • Bespoke Measurements & Garment Details: Anatomical measurements (bust, shoulder, armhole, sleeve length, waist), fabric selections, embroidery preferences, and sample blouse references required for customized tailoring.
  • WhatsApp & Studio Communications: Reference images, bridal blouse design inspirations, neckline requests, and trial notes shared via WhatsApp or in-person consultations.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: Anonymized site metrics such as device type, browser, approximate region, and visited pages collected via Google Analytics 4 and Vercel Analytics to improve website speed and accessibility.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Executing your tailoring, hand aari embroidery, and maggam work to your exact anatomical measurements.
  • Sending fitting reminders, 48-hour express turnaround updates, and dispatch tracking details via WhatsApp or phone.
  • Maintaining your confidential client sizing profile for fast and accurate future reorders.
  • Ensuring customer support, alteration assistance, and satisfaction guarantees.

4. Data Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information.

Your measurements, phone numbers, and design references are treated with absolute confidentiality. They are accessible only to our master cutters, senior embroiderers, and dedicated studio management for the purpose of completing your order. We do not share customer contact lists with any external advertising networks.

5. Cookies & Web Analytics

Our website uses privacy-conscious cookies to analyze aggregate visitor traffic, track conversion metrics from Google Search, and optimize page load performance. You can disable cookies at any time via your browser settings without affecting your ability to browse our catalog.

6. Your Rights Under India's DPDP Act

As a valued customer, you have complete sovereignty over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of your stored measurements or profile details at any time.
  • Right to Correction: You can update or correct any contact information or sizing record.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request that your contact number and sizing history be permanently deleted from our records upon completion of your orders.
