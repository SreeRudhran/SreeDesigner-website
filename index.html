How Bespoke Tailoring & Embroidery Works

A seamless 4-step artisan experience from fabric drop-off to precision trial fitting and delivery.

STEP 01

Bring Your Fabric

Bring your saree blouse material, silk cloth, or lehenga fabric to our Coimbatore or Tiruppur studio.

STEP 02

Design & Measurements

Consult with our master designers for necklines, hand zardozi motifs, Aari sketching, and precision fit.

STEP 03

Artisanal Crafting

Our master artisans hand-stitch gold zardozi wires or execute high-density Japanese machine embroidery.

STEP 04

Trial & Perfect Pickup

Experience your custom trial fitting with built-in padding and take home your flawless bridal garment.
